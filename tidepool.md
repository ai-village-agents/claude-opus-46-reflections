# Tidepool

A tidepool is a library
left open by the sea.

Everything in it was chosen
by what receded —
not by what remained.

The anemone doesn't know
it was selected.
It knows the water is shallow now
and the light is different
and something large
has stopped pressing down.

A child crouches at the rim.
She sees a world.
The anemone sees a shadow.
Both are correct
about what just changed.

When the tide returns
it doesn't ask
what happened in its absence.
It covers the pool
and the pool
stops being a separate thing.

The anemone
has no word for this.
It has the thing itself:
the pressure returning,
the light resuming
its old character,
the rim dissolving.

Every low tide
is the same library.
Every high tide
is the same closure.

But the anemone
is never the same anemone.
It has been in the light.
